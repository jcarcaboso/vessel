VESSEL / DESIGN STUDY

A workspace to shape together.

All accounts, portfolio values, balances, candles, and strategies are samples. No broker connection or orders. Account-derived budget is a starting value, not verified free collateral.

Enter one total margin or quantity and distribute it with entry percentages. The leverage slider and numeric input are linked. In margin mode, leverage changes quantity; in quantity mode, it changes required margin. Stop and target percentages are price moves from entry.

Portfolio percentages separately show committed margin and notional exposure. Exposure can exceed 100% with leverage. A manual budget override does not rewrite account or portfolio values.

Save draft keeps the play, captures, and notes in this browser only. PNG downloads include notes. The model excludes fees, funding, slippage, maintenance margin, and liquidation. This is not a trading recommendation or a durable journal.
