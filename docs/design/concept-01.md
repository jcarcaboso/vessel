# Play workspace concept 01

Initial visual direction, now represented by a disposable browser prototype after the owner's LAN-serving request. This is not an approved design or production application.

Historical reference. The owner subsequently locked the side-by-side layout and requested ten minimalist dark skins. The current comparison is at `/designs.html`; see [design studies](design-studies.md). The warm-light palette below is no longer the active direction.

## Composition

- Narrow navigation rail and a quiet Vessel header.
- Play title and Draft status, followed by portfolio, account, instrument, and direction.
- Large dark chart on the left, about two-thirds of the workspace.
- Entry editor on the right, with a play-level risk budget and independent entry cards.
- Selected entry, stop, and target highlighted on the chart. Other entries remain quieter.
- Thesis, strategy version, evidence, and review below the chart.
- Combined risk, reward-to-risk, and suggested size in a summary strip.

## Visual direction

Warm off-white workspace, white panels, fine sage borders, dark forest chart, and teal primary actions. Red and green distinguish stops and targets, with text labels as well. Avoid the density and neon colors of an exchange terminal.

## Illustrative content

A BTC long with two entries and a sample $25,000 account. A hypothetical 1% play risk is $250.

- Entry 1 at 64,200, stop at 63,000, target at 67,800, 60% of the risk budget.
- Entry 2 at 63,600, stop at 62,600, target at 66,600, 40% of the risk budget.

These are synthetic examples, not an investment recommendation. The browser prototype calculates this simplified linear case; see [its limits and verification](../prototype.md).

## Questions for the first review

- Is the chart large enough?
- Is the entry panel too narrow or too dense?
- Should thesis and strategy remain visible rather than sit in tabs?
- Does the quiet light workspace with a dark chart suit the product?
- What would you remove before adding anything?
