Six confirmed defects

Three high-priority execution problems and three material correction/editing problems. Each has reproduction inputs and an acceptance test in the written review.

R1 · P1 · Incomplete executions

Closed too early, open forever in the facts

A 2-unit entry order has only 1 retained entry fill. A 1-unit exit closes the Play. When the missing entry fill arrives, the status stays Closed with 1 unit still open.

Sizing counts the false win. Review excludes the incomplete result.
Required correction and evidence

Known order/fill completeness must gate closure and sizing. Keep the Play active with explicit incomplete-history coverage.

Reproduced in application-code probes; late fill ingestion was included. Backend B1.

R2 · P1 · Execution chronology

An old exit closes a later entry

A target-price sell from an earlier position fills before this Play's buy. The matcher links both and moves the new Play through Open to Closed.

10:02 sell, 10:04 buy cannot prove the new long has already exited.
Required correction and evidence

Check chronological exposure for planned targets and stops too. Early placement of a bracket is valid; an earlier exit fill is not a later entry's close.

Reproduced in application-code probes. Review rejects the chronology; sizing does not. Backend B2.

R3 · P1 · Fill meaning

Two buys can become a completed long

The API accepts a buy/open as the exit of a long. It reports entered 1, exited 1, open 0, while the actual signed quantity is long 2.

Real isolated API result: HTTP 200, status Closed.
Required correction and evidence

Validate side and normalized position effect when matching, confirming links and changing lifecycle. A closing-short fill must not automatically open a new long Play either.

Application probes and real HTTP/PostgreSQL reproduction. Receipt · Backend B3.

R4 · P2 · Terminal correction

Delete the link, then cannot restore it

The API removes an exit from a Closed Play. Status remains Closed but open quantity becomes 1. Relinking fails because the Play is already terminal.

Unlink: HTTP 200. Restore: HTTP 409. Normal Closed UI hides unlink, but a stale request can reach it.
Required correction and evidence

Reject terminal unlinking under the account lock. Preserve the existing final-status policy rather than inventing reopening.

Application probes and real HTTP/PostgreSQL reproduction. Backend B4.

R5 · P2 · Filled direction

A long's history can be revised to Short

A direction edit after an entry fill changes what side the matcher expects. Later real sell exits no longer match that filled long.

Filled entry prices and shares are protected; direction is not.
Required correction and evidence

Lock direction after entry fills, server-side and in the editor. Continue permitting ordinary stop and target revisions.

Reproduced through the application update service and reconciliation. Backend B5.

R6 · P2 · Silent note loss

Save can erase the note you are writing

Edit an image note while Save is pending. When the request completes, the older note replaces the edit and the interface says Saved.

Browser-confirmed with a real save/upload and a 10-second response delay.
Required correction and evidence

The reconciliation replaces the whole evidence object. Copy only server identity and saved metadata onto the current object; retain its note/markup and dirty state.

PlaysPage.tsx:109–118. Exact reproduction.

A passing existing test suite does not disprove these cases. The backend audit also records ten successful controls, including fee treatment, partial exits, initial-stop preservation and ambiguous concurrent Plays.

What should still be built

Complete the workflow

Manual fills and retrospective reviews

A manual account can plan a trade but has no execution-entry path to finish it. Imported operations also lack a supported retrospective journal workflow.

Record an actual manual buy at 100 and sell at 95. Keep explicit source/timestamps/fees. Imported history can receive a lesson without invented pre-trade intent or R.

Keep operations and Plays distinct; concurrent Plays can share a venue position.

Reuse existing arithmetic

A whole-position money summary

Add all-stops loss, all-targets gain, aggregate R:R and risk against a named reference. Include priced/stop coverage and optional cost assumptions.

Two tranches risk 0.5 quote units each: show total 1 before costs. If one stop is missing, show 0.5 known plus unknown risk, not 0.5 total.

The current Summary still contains just margin, notional, quantity and average entry.

Make learning possible

A short structured review

Extend the existing free-text review and Needs review queue with optional adherence, one mistake, a lesson and a next action.

A +2R win after breaking the risk plan is a win and a process violation. A planned −1R loss need not be a bad decision.

Collect reliable inputs before building setup rankings or plan-quality scores.

Complete money provenance

Funding and import coverage

History is already retained. Add scheduled ingestion, bounded backfill, gap detection and recovery. Separate execution fees, funding and other adjustments.

Hypothetical +120 gross −35 fees −110 funding = −25. Execution-only results are not funding-complete performance.

Do not invent funding allocations between simultaneous Plays. The official fill APIs have finite history windows.

Show actual protection

Stops with quantity and freshness

Answer whether a compatible live order covers the current position, not whether a stop line was drawn on the plan.

Position 1 BTC, live compatible stop 0.6 BTC: 0.4 is uncovered. A stale read is unknown protection, not a green badge.

Current per-Play checks are not a durable server-side monitor.

Respect concurrent exposure

Known and unknown portfolio risk

Show total known stop risk across Plays, unmatched venue exposure and unknown-stop counts. Keep the denominator explicit.

Three 150-unit risks on a 10,000-unit reference total 4.5% before costs, before any additional unknown exposure.

Start without correlation models, beta weights or false diversification claims.

Improve clarity, not the section positions

Preserve the approved desktop layout and shared responsive order. The current workspace is already more compact than the original review described.

Most useful UI changes

  1. Use the existing Summary for whole-position loss/gain/R:R.
  2. Turn Review exclusions into a list of affected Plays and repair reasons.
  3. Add title, instrument and account search to the Plays list.
  4. Separate "100% allocated" from "all entries priced and protected".
  5. Compress mobile context and keep save state accessible without moving sections.
  6. Offer account-first onboarding; a portfolio is optional.

What passed this time

  • Wrong-side stops block Save with a readable reason.
  • Leverage respects the loaded venue maximum.
  • Stops beyond estimated liquidation produce a warning.
  • New second entry gets a real 50/50 share split.
  • Expanded entry editor fits 320 px; Escape restores focus.
  • Review shows definitions, small samples and explicit exclusions.

No full screen-reader, touch-input or dense-chart-overlap certification is claimed.

Correct a risk tooltip: "Cross margin liquidates further away" is too absolute. Shared collateral and other positions matter. Say that cross liquidation depends on account-wide exposure and should be checked at the venue.

Nice to have, once the record is sound

Versioned strategies and checklists

Reuse the existing strategy design. Generic definitions, behavior rules, frozen versions and chart explanations. Do not mistake the current prototype for a shipped feature.

Templates, search and export

Duplicate a clean plan; save useful filters; export selected facts, notes and evidence. Operational backups already exist; portable journal exports are a different need.

Excursions and context snapshots

MAE/MFE from proven execution intervals, with candle-resolution limits. Timestamped market snapshots when planning and first filling, not fabricated context.

Weekly process review

A saved view of mistakes, adherence and next actions. Add external notifications only after durable monitoring and delivery semantics are defined.

Wait on AI trade grades, automatic recommendations, full backtesting, social feeds and more indicators. They interpret data before the important gaps in that data are fixed.
