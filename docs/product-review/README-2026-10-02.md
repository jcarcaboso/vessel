## The short version

1. **The loop isn't closed.** A Play cannot be saved, fills cannot be linked to plans, and nothing measures results in R. Closing *plan → execute → review → learn* comes before any new feature.
2. **Do the arithmetic.** Risk in $ and R, R:R, margin, fees, funding and liquidation versus the stop should be shown as labelled estimates, instead of "Not calculated". Example: at 25×, the BTC plan looks safe if everything fills, but if only the first entry fills, the position is liquidated before its stop.
3. **Validate the plan.** Wrong-side stops, shares that don't total 100% and leverage above the venue maximum are accepted silently today.
4. **Then learn:** fills → trades → R outcome → structured review → analytics that show sample size.
